What Opti-Moo simulates

1Crop and weather observations
→
2Root-zone water balance
→
3Health-preserving irrigation
→
4Pumping energy
→
5Grid-aware dispatch

The simulator advances soil-water storage hourly, estimates actual evapotranspiration and a fused crop-stress index, calculates the irrigation needed to protect the root zone, converts water to pumping energy using hydraulic head and pump efficiency, and schedules pumping according to the selected grid strategy.

Scientific-use disclosure

Opti-Moo is a research simulator. Default state values are deterministic observation-informed presets used to demonstrate the framework. Results become observation-driven only when users supply processed ECOSTRESS/OpenET inputs and appropriate groundwater and grid data. The yield-retention output is a transparent stress proxy, not a calibrated crop-yield forecast.
