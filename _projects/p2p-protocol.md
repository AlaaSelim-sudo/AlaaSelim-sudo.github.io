The Patient-to-Power Protocol connects patient surge, hospital operations, and energy-system constraints to support safer facility-level decisions during compound outage events.

The project aims to develop a transferable framework that connects clinical demand, building operation, backup power availability, thermal safety, emergency power priorities, and operator-facing summaries. The long-term goal is to support both urban and rural hospitals with practical decision-support tools during severe weather and grid disruptions.

**Keywords:** hospital energy resilience, patient safety, backup power, winter storms, critical facilities, decision support.
