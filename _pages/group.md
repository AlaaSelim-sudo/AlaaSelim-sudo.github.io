# Selim Research Group

Our group develops AI-assisted, physics-informed, and optimization-based methods for resilient energy systems. The work spans power-system restoration, cyber-physical security, data-center grid integration, grid-forming resources, and critical-facility energy resilience.

## Research directions

The group is currently organized around five connected research directions:

- **Resilient grid operation and restoration:** safe restoration, outage mitigation, and grid recovery under extreme events.
- **Cyber-physical security:** attack detection, mitigation, and secure control for active distribution networks and critical infrastructure.
- **AI and optimization for power systems:** reinforcement learning, model predictive control, physics-informed learning, and LLM-based decision support.
- **Data centers and grid flexibility:** data-center participation in restoration, grid services, dynamic hosting capacity, and energy-water-climate-aware operation.
- **Critical-facility energy resilience:** hospital energy management, patient-to-power decision support, backup power, and continuity of essential services.

## Prospective students and collaborators

I welcome motivated students who are interested in AI for power systems, cyber-physical security, data-center flexibility, grid restoration, hospital energy resilience, and critical infrastructure energy management.

Students can reach out with a short note describing their background, technical skills, and research interests.
