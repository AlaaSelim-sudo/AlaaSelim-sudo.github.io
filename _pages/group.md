# Selim Research Group

The Selim Research Group works on resilient energy systems, AI-assisted power-system operation, cyber-physical security, data-center grid integration, and critical-facility energy resilience.

The group brings together student researchers and mentees from multiple institutions. The structure below is organized by each student's home university and current research contribution.

## Prospective students and collaborators

I am interested in working with students and collaborators on AI for power systems, grid restoration, cyber-physical security, data-center flexibility, hospital energy resilience, and critical infrastructure decision support.

Students interested in these topics can reach out with a short description of their background, research interests, and technical skills.
