Alaa Selim

Postdoctoral Research Associate · Dartmouth Grid Intelligence Lab (GIL) AI · Power Systems · Cyber-Physical Security

Thayer School of Engineering at Dartmouth

Hanover, NH, USA

alaa.e.selim@dartmouth.edu

I am a postdoctoral researcher at Dartmouth’s Thayer School of Engineering and founder of the Grid Intelligence Lab (GIL) research direction. My work focuses on resilient energy systems, AI-assisted power-system operation, cyber-physical security, grid restoration, data-center grid integration, and critical-facility energy resilience.

My research asks how modern energy infrastructure can move from reactive recovery toward anticipatory, verified, and operator-friendly decision-making. I develop optimization, simulation, reinforcement learning, physics-informed machine learning, and hardware-in-the-loop workflows for power systems under outages, cyber-physical disturbances, extreme weather, and rapid large-load growth.

Resilient grid operation

Restoration, blackstart-compatible resources, microgrids, and secure operation of inverter-dominated distribution networks.

AI for power systems

Safe reinforcement learning, model-predictive control, physics-informed optimization, and LLM-based operator support.

Cyber-physical security

Attack detection, topology-control defense, communication-aware validation, and testbed-based security assessment.

Grid-aware infrastructure

Data centers, hospitals, and other critical facilities as coordinated, safety-aware energy resources during stress events.

Current research themes

  • Data centers as coordinated flexible assets for grid restoration and reliability.
  • Patient-to-Power decision support for hospital energy resilience during compound outages.
  • PowerGuard PG2 for multi-evidence cyber-physical defense of grid communication and control actions.
  • GridGuard for physics-informed LLM optimization and safer distribution-grid operation.
  • Grid-forming resources and dynamic hosting-capacity analysis for large flexible loads.
